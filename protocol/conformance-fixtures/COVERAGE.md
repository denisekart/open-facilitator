what a config-driven run of `protocol/tools/Conformance.Runner` against

(the Conformance.Runner scenario closes it — item/scenario ids reference
the runner config), `gap:env` (inherently a compose/environment proof —
stays with `reference/checks/*`), `deferred` (honest non-claim).
